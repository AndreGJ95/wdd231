Website Site Plan: Studio Urbana

W05 Project Site Plan

1. Site Name

Name: Studio Urbana

Reasoning: I chose this name because it reflects a modern and structured approach to architectural design, spatial planning, and urban landscape architecture, conveying professionalism and clarity to potential clients.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to showcase a professional portfolio of architectural design and interior remodeling services. It provides potential clients with detailed information regarding past projects, working methodologies, estimation guidelines, and a direct contact form to request consultations and quotes.

3. Target Audience Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: "Where can I view previous residential remodeling renders and floor plans to evaluate the designer's style and quality before requesting a quote?"
  • Scenario 2: "What information and technical documents do I need to prepare before scheduling an initial consultation for a construction permit or renovation?"

4. Color Schema

The chosen color palette for the website, which is also applied throughout this site plan document, consists of the following colors:

Primary: Dark Navy (#1E3A8A)
Secondary: Emerald Green (#059669)
Accent: Light Neutral (#F3F4F6)

Application: Dark Navy is used for main headers and navigation areas to build trust and authority. Emerald Green is used for call-to-action buttons, accents, and highlighted links. Light Neutral serves as the background color to maintain readability and clean whitespace.

5. Typography

Two primary fonts were selected from Google Fonts and applied across this document:

  • Montserrat (Headings): Applied to all major section titles (H1, H2, H3) to establish a clean, modern visual hierarchy.
  • Roboto (Body Text): Applied to paragraphs, list items, and standard copy for optimal readability on digital displays.
